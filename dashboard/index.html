Implied value is this radar's own claim from the snapshot's figures — never make.fun's, never DefiLlama's. “Records no token” means the snapshot records no token for the protocol: both gecko_id and symbol are null. A protocol that carries a symbol (MORPHO, ENA, SYRUP…) already records a token and sits in the Live view, never on the ranked board. Peer token share is the share of same-age protocols that already record a token: information only, it never changes a rank. Farm figures assume a 5% airdrop pool, a $2,000 standard position and 4 active hours; a smaller deposit earns proportionally less.

Data: DefiLlama public API, fetched once by the house. Snapshot date . Model: least squares of log market cap on log TVL and clipped 7-day change, fit on the 128 live protocols that record a market cap. Built offline; no network.
